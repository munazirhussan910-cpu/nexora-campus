# NEXORA CAMPUS — 3-MINUTE HACKATHON JUDGING DEMO GUIDE

This walkthrough guarantees a seamless 3-minute presentation for hackathon evaluators.

---

## ⚡ Preparation
1. Ensure both frontend and backend are running:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
2. Open your browser to: **[http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)**
3. Ensure the floating **Demo Persona Switcher** widget is visible on the bottom-right corner.

---

## ⏱️ Step-by-Step Demo Script (3 Minutes)

### Minute 1: Complaint Lifecycle & Deterministic Routing (Aryan &rarr; Ramesh)
1. **Student Complaint**:
   - In the Persona Switcher (bottom right), click **Aryan (Student)**.
   - Navigate to **+ New Complaint** or click the quick action button on the dashboard.
   - Type Title: `Tap Leakage in bathroom washbasin`.
   - Notice the live routing box instantaneously updates:
     > *"Auto-routing category: **Plumbing** • Assigned: **Ramesh Kumar (Senior Plumber)**"*
   - Click **Submit Complaint**. The ticket (e.g., `NX-10108`) is created and assigned.
2. **Technician Fulfillment**:
   - Click **Ramesh (Plumber)** on the Persona Switcher.
   - Open ticket `NX-10108` in the Work Orders queue.
   - Click **Accept Task** &rarr; state changes to `ACCEPTED`.
   - Click **Start On-Site Work** &rarr; state changes to `IN_PROGRESS`.
   - In the resolution notes box, enter: *"Replaced worn spindle gasket with brand new brass washer and tested water pressure."*
   - Click **Mark Resolved & Notify Student**.
3. **Student Rating**:
   - Switch back to **Aryan (Student)**.
   - Open ticket `NX-10108`. Confirm the resolution and click **5 Stars** with feedback!

---

### Minute 2: Gate Pass Cryptography & Document Verification (Aryan &rarr; Warden &rarr; Security)
1. **Gate Pass Request**:
   - As **Aryan**, click **Gate Pass (QR & PIN)**.
   - Destination: *Bhubaneswar City Market*, Reason: *Project supplies*.
   - Click **Submit Gate Pass Request**.
2. **Warden Digital Approval**:
   - Click **Warden (Block B)** in the Persona Switcher.
   - Review the application in the pending list and click **Approve Pass**.
   - The backend signs the pass with HMAC, generates a cryptographic QR token, and issues a 4-digit PIN (e.g. `7392`).
3. **Gate Security Officer Check**:
   - Click **Security (Gate)** in the Persona Switcher.
   - Click **OPEN GATE SCANNER (QR / PIN)**.
   - Click the preset button **PIN 7392 (Aryan - Ready)** &rarr; click **VERIFY**.
   - Verification status: `GATE PASS IS VALID & AUTHORIZED`.
   - Click **MARK STUDENT DEPARTED** &rarr; student is logged as exited campus!
   - Click **MARK STUDENT RETURNED** &rarr; student is logged as returned and pass is safely closed.
4. **Official Bonafide PDF & Public Verification**:
   - Switch to **Chief Admin** &rarr; go to **Approvals Hub**.
   - Approve Aryan's pending Bonafide request.
   - Click **Download PDF** to inspect the server-rendered PDF generated by PDFKit with official BPUT seals and verification QR!
   - Click **Verify Online** &rarr; public URL `/verify/NX-BON-2026-00199` verifies document legitimacy!

---

### Minute 3: Operational Cockpit, Section 44 Recurring Issues & Lite Mode
1. **Command Center Cockpit**:
   - In **Chief Admin**, go to **Command Center**.
   - Highlight live real-time metrics:
     - Open Requests
     - Overdue Breaches
     - SLA Ageing Distribution (`< 12h`, `12-24h`, `24-48h`, `> 48h`)
2. **Section 44 Recurring Issue Alert**:
   - Point out the prominent red alert card:
     > **OPERATIONAL ALERT: RECURRING ISSUE DETECTED (SECTION 44)**
     > **Hostel Block B • Category: Plumbing • 7 Complaints in 14 Days**
     > *"Recommendation: Conduct systematic inspection of Block B plumbing infrastructure. Multiple recurring failures detected."*
3. **Low-Bandwidth Nexora Lite**:
   - In the Persona Switcher, click **Nexora Lite** (or go to `/lite`).
   - Demonstrate the clean, text-first, high-performance interface running under 25 KB for 2G/3G campus connections!
